# Las Juntas house - concept 03

Schematic study, 12 September 2026. This option develops the client's proposed bathroom/laundry swap and a short interior walkway. Concept 02 is preserved for comparison.

## Client requirements retained

- 10 x 20 m lot, with 10 m frontage on Alvaro Obregon, Las Juntas, Puerto Vallarta.
- Two floors, three bedrooms and two full shared bathrooms; up to eight guests.
- Queen guest room downstairs; king primary and two-double guest room upstairs.
- Downstairs bathroom separated from the kitchen.
- One car, rear patio, no pool; warm Mexican contemporary materials.
- MXN 3-5 million excluding land; provisional working target MXN 4 million, subject to local pricing.
- PDF tables use separate meter and feet reference columns, and square meters / square feet for areas. Metric dimensions govern.

## Front service area

The bathroom and laundry exchange sides, with a partition adjustment. G2 occupies the left side and retains its 2.00 x 2.30 m internal planning footprint. G3 is a recessed 1.45 x 1.30 m laundry bay nearer G6, the entry hall. G8 is a separate 1.45 m-long by 1.00 m-wide indoor passage behind that bay. It opens from G6 and leads to the bathroom door.

The route is G6 entry hall -> G8 passage -> G2 bathroom. G3 opens directly onto G8 for indoor laundry access. The former exterior laundry door is removed. The side passage remains an outdoor service route, but is no longer required to reach laundry.

## Laundry assumption and tradeoff

This option assumes a stacked washer/dryer, shown with a nominal 0.75 x 0.85 m footprint, plus a small utility counter/sink. The laundry bay is open to the passage; no enclosure doors are drawn. Select equipment and verify depth, stacking height, hoses, electrical supply, drains and dryer ventilation before fixing the joinery. Any later laundry enclosure must be designed around those clearances and preserve the passage width. Loading laundry temporarily uses part of G8.

The bathroom retains a 1.00 x 0.90 m shower, a toilet and a 0.90 m vanity. The ground laundry window shifts to the small utility-counter position; bathroom glazing should have an appropriate privacy sill/treatment. The appearance board remains an earlier style reference and does not establish these window details.

## Other rooms, areas and cost

The downstairs bedroom, kitchen and social rooms, stairs and complete upper floor retain their concept 02 geometry. The bathroom's rear partition still ends at y=2.65; the kitchen zone starts at y=4.65. The resulting 2.00 m separation is a plan-depth measure, not a walking distance or regulatory minimum. The bathroom has no kitchen wall in common.

Ground exterior envelope: 94.5 m². Upper envelope: 85.5 m², less 7.4025 m² stair opening, giving 78.0975 m². Combined floor area: 172.5975 m². Covered terrace: separate 18 m². Lot allocation remains 94.5 + 55 + 10.5 + 40 = 200 m². These are schematic area conventions, not an approved municipal calculation.

The provisional MXN 4 million budget remains unchanged: construction/site works 2.6 million; design/studies/permits 0.3 million; furniture/appliances/linens 0.5 million; contingency 0.6 million. Reprice partitions, fixtures and services locally; this rearrangement does not establish a saving or prove affordability.
