# Las Juntas house - concept 06

Schematic revision, 12 September 2026. Implements the approved materials and noise-control direction for potential street traffic and general household noise. Concept 05 is preserved. This is a planning and pricing brief, not a selected acoustic system or engineered structural design.

## Layout changes

- G5: TV and media console move from the downstairs bedroom wall to the left exterior wall. A 2.60 x 0.85 m three-seat sofa faces the TV, with two separate chairs. The dining table still seats eight. A nominal 1.00 m route runs beside the sofa and dining furniture and connects to the rear circulation space and terrace doors. Final furniture pull-out and operating clearances need coordination.
- D1: new 0.90 m nominal hinged door at the G6/G7 stair entrance. Its leaf swings into G6, outside the front stair landing. The surrounding stair walls must form a continuous enclosure for acoustic separation.
- D0: existing 1.00 m front entrance shifts 0.20 m left in building-local coordinates, from x=4.30 to x=4.10. Its nominal sweep envelope and D1's are separated by 0.225 m. This is a geometric check, not an accessible-route or finished hardware certification.
- D2: new 0.90 m nominal door at G6/G8, swinging into G6. It encloses the laundry bay G3 and service passage G8 together. Ordinary cabinet leaves in the shallow G3 bay would obstruct the bathroom route, so the acoustic closure is placed at the service-vestibule entrance instead. The machines remain in an alcove inside that enclosure.
- G8 remains 1.45 x 1.00 m. Neither D2 nor the inward-opening G2 bathroom door sweeps into it. The hall doorway is 0.90 m nominal; final clear opening will depend on the frame and hardware. Appliance loading still temporarily uses G8. Ventilation, heat removal and exhaust must follow the selected appliances and be coordinated with acoustic control.

## Construction and acoustic scope

Proposed starting construction: engineer-designed reinforced-concrete structure and foundations, concrete masonry infill, concrete upper floor and roof, with warm plaster/stucco, porcelain or ceramic tile and terracotta accents. Wall roles, soil/foundation design, beams, slab sizes, reinforcement, site levels and local compliance remain unresolved.

- AW1: complete acoustic wall assemblies, with full-height separation, sealed perimeters/penetrations and controlled structural connections. Mineral wool or blockwork alone does not establish a performance rating.
- AD1: sealed solid-core hinged doors for bedrooms, the stair and the service vestibule; compatible perimeter and drop seals, frames and hardware. Coordinate ventilation and pressure paths.
- AV1: complete bedroom window assemblies tested for the actual traffic spectrum; acoustic laminated glazing is a candidate. Coordinate solar/thermal performance, quiet closed-window ventilation and cooling.
- AF1: tile-compatible impact isolation with perimeter isolation, engineered concrete floor, and services/isolated ceiling where needed. Obtain full floor/ceiling test evidence for airborne and impact performance separately.
- AP1: appliance-specific laundry ventilation and heat/exhaust paths, vibration-isolated equipment/supports, and plumbing routed away from bedroom linings where practical. Pumps and condensers need locations protecting this house and neighbors. Routes and equipment locations are not yet drawn.
- Soft furnishings address reverberation. Front wall/gate treatment is a later item after site listening/measurement and pricing. No sound reduction, barrier height or numeric performance target is claimed.
