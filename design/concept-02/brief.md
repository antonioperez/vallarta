# Las Juntas house - concept 02

Schematic revision, 12 September 2026. This is the current design; concept 01 remains available for comparison.

## Client requirements

- 10 x 20 m lot with 10 m frontage on Alvaro Obregon in Las Juntas, Puerto Vallarta.
- Two-story vacation house, three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, sleeping up to eight.
- Downstairs queen guest room; upstairs king primary and guest room with two double beds.
- The downstairs bathroom must not be next to the kitchen.
- One car, rear patio, no pool. Warm Mexican contemporary with selective traditional tilework.
- Project budget excluding land: MXN 3-5 million, working target MXN 4 million; still requires local pricing.
- PDF schedules show separate meters and feet reference columns, with square meters and square feet for areas. Drawing dimensions remain metric.

## Revised arrangement

Ground: move the shared full bathroom and laundry into the front-left service area. The bathroom is 2.00 x 2.30 m, opens from the entry hall and shares no wall with the kitchen. In building-local coordinates the bathroom occupies x=1.8..3.8, y=0.2..2.5; its rear partition ends at y=2.65 and the kitchen zone starts at y=4.65. The 2.00 m difference is plan-depth separation, not a walking-distance or regulatory minimum. The bedroom and circulation separate these uses. No bathroom door opens directly into the kitchen. The entry bench sits against the stair wall, clear of the relocated bedroom doorway.

The queen bedroom moves behind the bathroom/laundry but retains its 3.60 x 4.10 m room size. Daylight and an operable window face the side passage, subject to local approval. Kitchen, dining for eight, lounge and rear patio remain in the rear social area. Laundry is accessed from the side passage and now measures 1.45 x 2.30 m.

Upper: replace both en suites with one hall-accessed family bathroom at the front, above the ground service area. Its 3.60 x 2.30 m envelope includes a double vanity, shower and a toilet compartment inside the same bathroom. This is one full bathroom with one toilet, not a separate third bathroom. Internal partitions and fixtures need detailed local review. The wet areas are grouped vertically but fixture locations are not all directly aligned; provide a designed service chase and drain routing.

The four-person guest bedroom moves behind the bathroom and uses two double beds with heads toward the rear wall. It is 4.60 x 4.10 m, with a front wardrobe and access from the hall. The primary remains at the rear with a 3.85 x 4.65 m bedroom and a separate 4.60 x 2.40 m dressing/storage room. A small hall linen cupboard is shared. Both upstairs bedrooms reach the bathroom without passing through the other bedroom.

Use privacy glazing at the front bathroom and laundry windows. The appearance board from concept 01 is retained only as a material/massing reference; it has not been updated for the new front-room windows.

## Geometry and cost

Building/site geometry is unchanged: ground exterior envelope 9 x 10.5 m = 94.5 m², upper exterior envelope 9 x 9.5 m = 85.5 m² less 7.4025 m² stair opening = 78.0975 m². Combined floor area measured to external wall faces with the upper opening deducted: 172.5975 m². Covered terrace is a separate 18 m².

Lot allocation: ground footprint 94.5 + front zone 55 + side passage 10.5 + rear outdoor zone 40 = 200 m². The nominal 1 m side passage and construction along the opposite boundary remain conditional on the surveyed and permitted building envelope. No surveyed north, floor elevation, foundation or code compliance is asserted.

The existing provisional MXN 4 million allocation remains: construction/site works 2.6 million, design/studies/permits 0.3 million, furnishings/appliances/linens 0.5 million, contingency 0.6 million. One bathroom is removed, but the house area is unchanged and the family bathroom is larger. Do not claim a fixed saving without repricing the changed scope. These allowances are not quotations or evidence of affordability.
