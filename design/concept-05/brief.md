# Las Juntas house - concept 05

Schematic revision, 12 September 2026. The client has adopted 3.00 m clear ceilings downstairs, 2.80 m upstairs, and a provisional 3.40 m finished floor-to-floor height. This version resolves the stair geometry for that rise and adds a vertical height study. Concept 04 is preserved.

## Adopted heights

Clear height is measured from finished floor to finished ceiling. The ground finished floor is the relative +0.00 m datum; the upper finished floor is +3.40 m. The underside of the ground ceiling is +3.00 m, and the upper ceiling is +6.20 m. The 0.40 m interval between the lower ceiling and upper finished floor is an allowance for the complete floor assembly, including structure, finishes and services. It is not a specified concrete slab thickness. The engineer and architect must coordinate beams and services to maintain the adopted clear heights.

Ground-floor elevation above actual grade, roof structure, roof insulation and drainage build-up, parapets, and the final overall permitted building height are not fixed. The roof band in the height diagram is an undimensioned graphic placeholder. The prior appearance rendering has not been regenerated for these adopted heights.

## Stair revision and its effect on the plan

The former provisional 18-riser stair for a 3.15 m rise is replaced by 20 risers of 0.170 m, divided into two flights of 10 risers. Each flight has nine 0.280 m treads, giving a 2.52 m run. The turning landing is at +1.70 m, and the upper arrival is at +3.40 m.

The nominal flights and turning landing are 1.05 m wide/deep; the front landing is 1.00 m deep. A flight, front landing and turning landing require 1.00 + 2.52 + 1.05 = 4.57 m. The reserved bay increases from 2.50 x 4.30 m to 2.50 x 4.60 m, retaining a 0.03 m nominal plan allowance. Finished widths and handrail projections are not yet detailed.

The bay extends 0.30 m toward the rear in building-local coordinates. Its back edge moves from y=4.50 to y=4.80, with the rear partition occupying y=4.80-4.95. The kitchen counter and fridge move 0.30 m rearward, and the primary bedroom's front wall/door move by the same amount. The existing king bed and bedside furniture stay in place.

## Room arrangement retained

The house remains a two-story, three-bedroom, two-full-bathroom vacation home for up to eight people on a 10 x 20 m lot in Las Juntas, with 10 m frontage on Alvaro Obregon. One car, a rear patio and covered terrace, and the warm Mexican contemporary material direction remain in the brief. No pool is included.

Downstairs, G2 has a shower, toilet and vanity. G3 is an open recessed stacked-laundry bay, reached indoors through the 1.00 m-wide G8 passage from G6. The bathroom door is at the end of that passage and swings inward. The bathroom, laundry and passage retain concept 04 geometry. The bathroom still has no shared wall with the kitchen; the rear-wall-to-kitchen plan-depth separation is now 2.30 m because the kitchen zone starts at y=4.95 rather than y=4.65. This is a geometric measure, not a regulatory minimum.

Upstairs, U2 remains one open shared bathroom without a separate toilet compartment. Its far-end shower is 1.10 x 2.30 m, opposite the hall entrance. The toilet and double vanity are in the main room. Both upstairs guest-room layout and primary dressing/storage retain their previous geometry. Bathroom fixtures and access are unchanged from concept 04.

## Areas and budget

Exterior floor envelopes and site allocation remain unchanged. Ground footprint: 94.5 m². Upper envelope: 85.5 m², less the revised 8.0775 m² stair opening, giving 77.4225 m². Combined floor area is 171.9225 m², displayed as 171.9 m² (1,850.6 ft²). The 0.675 m² difference from concept 04 is due to the larger stair opening. Covered terrace: separate 18 m². Lot allocation remains 94.5 + 55 + 10.5 + 40 = 200 m². These are schematic area conventions; municipal accounting may differ.

The budget remains a provisional MXN 3-5 million excluding land, with a MXN 4 million working allocation: construction/site works 2.6 million; design/studies/permits 0.3 million; furniture/appliances/linens 0.5 million; contingency 0.6 million. Taller walls, the revised stair, structural coordination and bathroom scope need local repricing. The area reduction is not evidence of a cost saving.
