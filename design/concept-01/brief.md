# Las Juntas house - concept 01

Prepared 12 September 2026. Schematic study for discussion; not a permit or construction drawing.

## Confirmed brief

- Lot: 10 m frontage x 20 m depth; frontage on Alvaro Obregon, Las Juntas, Puerto Vallarta.
- User-provided pin: 20 degrees 41 minutes 59.51 seconds N, 105 degrees 14 minutes 44.63 seconds W (20.69986389, -105.24573056).
- Vacation use; three bedrooms sleeping eight; two floors; one car; patio; no pool.
- Upper primary with king bed and private bathroom; upper guest room with two double beds and private bathroom; ground guest room with queen bed and shared full bathroom.
- Warm Mexican contemporary with selective traditional tilework.
- Total project budget excluding land: MXN 3-5 million. Working target: MXN 4 million, not an established construction estimate.

- Lot: x=0..10, y=0..20.
- Ground building exterior envelope: x=1..10, y=5.5..16; 9 x 10.5 = 94.5 square meters.
- Upper exterior envelope: x=1..10, y=5.5..15; 9 x 9.5 = 85.5 square meters before deducting the stair opening.
- Ground/upper local room coordinates use the building front-left corner (site x=1, y=5.5) as their origin.

- Rear outdoor zone = 40 square meters, including covered terrace 18 and uncovered space 22. Side access = 10.5 square meters, nominally 1 m wide. Front zone = 55 square meters.

## Fit and operational choices

- Queen reference: 1.52 x 2.03 m; king: 2.00 x 2.00 m; each double: 1.35 x 1.90 m. Confirm actual purchased mattress sizes. Eight-person capacity assumes two people sharing each double bed.
- Indoor dining table: 2.20 x 0.95 m with eight chairs. L-shaped kitchen; no island.
- Lounge: L-shaped sofa plus two armchairs; final seat count depends on selected furniture. Dining chairs can supplement seating at full occupancy.
- Primary dressing/storage is a separate room. Secure owner storage can be fitted within this room if vacation rentals are later considered; independent rental accommodation is not included.
- Laundry is accessed from the side passage through an exterior sliding service door. It contains a stacked washer/dryer and a utility sink. This preserves internal social space.
- The ground bathroom door opens to the circulation area, away from the main dining table. No bathroom is accessed through another bedroom.
- Bathrooms are clustered in the same part of the two floors, but are not perfectly fixture-aligned. Allow an accessible service chase; engineer final drain slopes and ventilation.
- Stair study: U-shaped, 18 risers total, 9 per flight. A provisional 3.15 m floor-to-floor height gives 175 mm risers; 8 treads per flight at 280 mm give a 2.24 m run. Nominal flight width 1.05 m, intermediate landing depth 1.05 m, front landing 1.00 m. The 2.50 x 4.30 m stair zone reserves space; headroom, guards, rail clearances, structure, egress and local compliance remain for the architect/engineer.
- Upper floor stops 1 m short of the ground rear wall. This lower roof strip is maintenance-only; no roof terrace or balcony. Roof slopes, overflow and service access must be designed.
- Exterior openings are confined to front, rear and the side-passage elevation. The right boundary wall has no openings.
- Fans, operable screened windows and zoned air-conditioning; kitchen/bath/laundry extraction to a designed outdoor termination. Equipment sizes and utility capacities remain unselected.
- No floor level, foundation, structural span, member size, seismic/wind detailing, flood classification or code compliance is established by this concept.

## Budget control - allocations, not market prices

At a MXN 4 million total working target, provisionally reserve MXN 600,000 (15%) as contingency, MXN 300,000 for design/studies/permits and MXN 500,000 for furniture, appliances and linens. The residual MXN 2,600,000 is the allowance available for construction, installed kitchen/joinery, services, drainage, gate/boundaries and the terrace. The non-construction allowances must be replaced by actual proposals. Tax, delivery, installation and scope inclusions must be explicit in quotations.

The 173 square meter concept has not been shown to fit the residual allowance. Price it before approving design development. If it does not fit, first simplify discretionary finishes and joinery; then develop a smaller, furnished alternative that preserves three bedrooms, three baths and outdoor usefulness. Do not reduce structure, drainage or waterproofing quality to meet the budget.
