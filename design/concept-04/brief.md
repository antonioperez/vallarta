# Las Juntas house - concept 04

Schematic revision, 12 September 2026. This version removes the upstairs toilet compartment and puts the shower at the far end of the shared bathroom. Concept 03 remains available for comparison.

## Current brief

- 10 x 20 m lot, with 10 m frontage on Alvaro Obregon, Las Juntas, Puerto Vallarta.
- Two floors, three bedrooms and two full shared bathrooms, sleeping up to eight.
- Downstairs queen bedroom; upstairs king primary and two-double guest bedroom.
- Downstairs bathroom separated from the kitchen.
- Indoor access to downstairs laundry and bathroom through the short G8 passage.
- One car, rear patio, no pool; warm Mexican contemporary appearance.
- Provisional budget MXN 3-5 million excluding land, with MXN 4 million as the working target; subject to local pricing.
- PDF schedules retain separate meters and feet reference columns, plus square meters / square feet for areas. Metric dimensions govern.

## Upstairs bathroom revision

The user's reference to the bathroom's own WC/MC is interpreted as the enclosed toilet compartment, not removal of the toilet. U2 remains one full shared bathroom with one toilet, one double vanity and one shower. The internal WC wall and its separate door are removed.

The far end is the left end of U2, opposite the hall entrance at the right. It is not the rear of the house. The new walk-in shower occupies the former toilet side of the room and runs along the left wall. This study uses a shower, which was one of the two bathing fixtures the user allowed.

The shower measures 1.10 x 2.30 m (3.61 x 7.55 ft). Its glass line is at x=1.30, with a nominal 0.90 m opening along y=0.90-1.80. The toilet is in the open dry area, facing the double vanity. Its nominal front-to-vanity separation is 0.97 m before selecting fixtures and detailing finishes. This is a geometric measurement, not a code or accessibility determination.

The hall entrance remains on the right at x=3.875, y=0.85, with a nominal 0.90 m inward door swing. The drawn door sweep does not intersect any fixture rectangle. Shower glazing and entry, splash control, ventilation, drainage, waterproofing, fixture mounting and finished clearances require detailed design. The shower lies against the shared bedroom wall; service routing and acoustic treatment should be coordinated there. Plumbing positions change within the existing front service area and are not a finalized vertical stack design.

## Downstairs arrangement retained

G2 remains on the left of the front service area at 2.00 x 2.30 m. G3 is an open recessed laundry bay, 1.45 x 1.30 m, containing a nominal stacked washer/dryer and small utility counter/sink. G8 is a 1.45 m-long, 1.00 m-wide passage behind the laundry, accessed from G6. It leads to the bathroom and opens directly onto the laundry bay. No exterior trip is needed to use the laundry.

The bathroom's nominal 0.85 m door swings inward. Stacked-machine dimensions, hose space, utilities, dryer ventilation and any future laundry enclosure need to follow selected equipment. Loading temporarily uses part of the passage. The exterior laundry entrance remains removed.

The ground bathroom shares no wall with the kitchen. Its rear partition ends at y=2.65; the kitchen starts at y=4.65. That 2.00 m difference is plan-depth separation, not walking distance or a regulatory minimum. The bedroom and circulation buffer these uses.
