## The new kitchen wall

The extension `(5.20, 4.80, 1.10, 0.15)` reaches the 3.00 m ceiling and joins the stair rear wall. This is a useful architectural location to coordinate a potential structural line, but the extension was introduced to back the cooking counter. No foundation, reinforcement, beam connection, material strength or bearing role was added.

Treat it as **bearing role unassigned** until the structural design explicitly classifies it. If it is a partition, design its own support, anchorage and interaction with the frame. If it is selected as bearing or lateral-resisting masonry, engineer the complete wall, openings, reinforcement/confinement and foundation. A full-height block wall or a few castillos do not automatically constitute an engineered concrete frame. Avoid drilling, chasing or placing hood/services through eventual beams/columns without coordinated details.

## Current municipal references checked

The [municipal register](https://transparencia.puertovallarta.gob.mx/articulo8.php?pag=art8-secIId), accessed 13 September 2026, lists the construction regulation as currently in force, last amended 29 October 2020. Its linked consolidated [construction regulation and technical provisions](https://transparencia.puertovallarta.gob.mx/transparenciaY/normatividad/reglamentos/2023/REGLAMENTO%20DE%20CONSTRUCCI%C3%93N%20PARA%20EL%20MUNICIPIO%20DE%20PUERTO%20VALLARTA%2C%20JALISCO%20Y%20SUS%20NORMAS%20TECNICAS%20COMPLEMENTARIAS..doc) were downloaded and read.

- Articles 149/195: boundary separation; 5 cm is a minimum, with further seismic displacement/height rules.
- Articles 151/159/164: nonstructural elements, dead/live/seismic/wind actions and participation in lateral resistance.
- Article 178: structural drawings, calculation documentation and professional signatures.
- Articles 202-204: material design provisions; concrete references include the cited Mexico City/ACI route and seismic detailing. The designer must establish the applicable editions and accepted basis.
- Articles 205-208: foundation and geotechnical requirements, including an authorized geotechnical professional.

This review does not select a wind speed, seismic coefficient, soil class, member size, reinforcement ratio, footing size or technical-standard edition for this parcel. Those require the design basis and site evidence.
